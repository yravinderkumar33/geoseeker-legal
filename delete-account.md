# Delete Your GeoSeeker Account

GeoSeeker (`co.geoseeker.app`), developed by Ravinder Kumar, lets you delete your account and the data associated with it at any time. You can do this yourself inside the app, or request deletion by email.

## Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

1. Open the GeoSeeker app and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings** (the gear icon).
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Type `DELETE` to confirm, then confirm the deletion.

Your account and its data are deleted immediately.

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email **[yravinderkumar33@gmail.com](mailto:yravinderkumar33@gmail.com)** from the email address associated with your GeoSeeker account with the subject line "Delete my account". We will verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

## What gets deleted

- Your account and sign-in identity (email / Google / Apple link).
- Your username.
- Your cloud progress backup (completed hunts, wishlist, streak).

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We keep no copies.

## What is not deleted

- Game progress stored locally on your device (passport stamps, settings). Uninstalling the app removes it.
- Anonymous analytics events (if you opted in) — these use a random identifier and are not linked to your account, so they cannot be traced back to you.
