# Bishop: World Hunt Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 July 2026_

Bishop: World Hunt is a daily travel hide-and-seek game. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. The short version: your game lives on your device; an account and analytics are optional, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

## Data stored on your device

Your game progress (completed hunts, passport stamps, wishlist, streak, settings, and any hunt in progress) is stored locally on your device. If you never sign in, it never leaves your device.

## Account data (optional)

If you choose to sign in (Google, Apple, or email one-time code), we store with our backend provider (Supabase):

- Your account identifier and email address (for sign-in only).
- Your chosen username (shown on results you choose to share).
- A backup of your game progress (completed hunts, wishlist, streak), so it can be restored on a new device.

We do not access your contacts, photos, or precise device location. The game's map interactions are game guesses, not your location — Bishop: World Hunt never requests device location permissions.

## Analytics (optional, anonymous)

To tune clue difficulty and fairness, the app can send anonymous gameplay events (for example, "a guess was made and it was Hot", "a hint was offered") to PostHog. These events use a random anonymous identifier, are not linked to your account, are never used for advertising, and are never sold. You can turn this off at any time in **Settings → Share anonymous usage data**.

## Notifications (optional)

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. No push notification service receives your data.

## Third-party services

- **Supabase** (authentication and progress backup, only if you sign in).
- **PostHog** (anonymous analytics, unless you opt out).
- **CARTO / OpenStreetMap** (map tiles: your device requests map imagery from CARTO's servers, which involves your IP address as with any web request).

## Data deletion

You can delete your account from inside the app: **Settings → Delete account**, then type `DELETE` to confirm. This permanently erases your account, your username, and your cloud progress backup from our systems. It cannot be undone, and it happens immediately — we keep no copies.

Deleting your account does not remove the game progress stored on your device; deleting the app removes that.

If you cannot reach the in-app flow (for example, you can no longer sign in), email us at the address below and we will erase the account for you. See [Delete Your Account](../delete-account/) for details.

## Children

Bishop: World Hunt is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
